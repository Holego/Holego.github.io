2. Permissions and Data Collected by the Apps

The Apps request the following device permissions to function:

  • Storage — used to save game data, settings, and other App files on the device.
  • Internet access — used to display ads and enable analytics (see Section 3).

The Apps do not use the device's camera or microphone.

Files created by the Apps are stored locally on the user's device and are not transmitted to the developer's servers.

These services may collect data such as device identifiers (Advertising ID), approximate location, device type, OS version, ad interaction data, and app crash reports. The processing of this data is governed by the respective privacy policies of these services:

forgotten-island-4 — in development

Forgotten Island 4 — fantasy action RPG

A story-driven action RPG built in Godot 4 for solo play, co-op and a persistent shared world. Play the campaign alone or with up to eight allies through levels 1–60, then step into the online world. It is a playable prototype in active development: systems are built, tested and documented one by one.

Godot 4.7GDScriptMultiplayerBlenderIn development

The source code is in a private repository. The in-game UI is currently in Russian.

  • Real-time combat: class resources and abilities (warrior rage, mage, rogue combo points), directional weapon motions, status effects and hit feedback.
  • Enemy AI with a threat system; bosses with telegraphed attacks (charge, ground slam, grab-and-throw) and group boss encounters.
  • Procedurally generated dungeon floors built from room sockets, plus a death and resurrection flow.
  • A streamed open-world prototype with a world map.
  • Server-authoritative co-op and MMO foundations: accounts backend, dedicated zone server, trading, mounts, chat.
  • Quests, dialogues, shops, interactive mechanisms (levers, code doors, pressure plates) and environment zones (water, sand, wind, vortex).
